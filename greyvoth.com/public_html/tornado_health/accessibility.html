Our commitment

Everyone on a Tornado Health plan should be able to check coverage, follow claims, find care and reach us on their own terms. We design and test the member portal to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What's built in

  • Keyboard: everything works without a mouse, in a logical order, with a skip link to the main content.
  • Visible focus: a 3px outline shows where you are on every control.
  • Screen readers: headings, landmarks and labels on every page; changes like search results, saved settings and new messages are announced.
  • Contrast: text meets 4.5:1 and controls 3:1 against their backgrounds, including on the purple bands.
  • Room to tap: buttons and links are at least 24px, and most are 48px.
  • Zoom and small screens: pages work at up to 400% browser zoom and on screens as narrow as 320px, without sideways scrolling.
  • Calm motion: animations are short, and they're off if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Steady pages: content doesn't jump around while it loads.
  • Plain language: short sentences and everyday words, with the Help center for terms like deductible and EOB.

How we check

Every page, and states like errors, open panels and search results, is checked with automated accessibility testing (axe-core) and HTML validation. We also check keyboard use, focus order, and layouts from 320px to wide screens. See every check and its result.

Known limits

  • The forms in the Help center are placeholders in this demo, so they aren't tagged PDFs yet.
  • AI Chat answers from sample information and can't give medical advice.
  • Some links lead to pages that aren't part of the demo yet; they say so instead of going nowhere.

Other formats and languages

We can send letters, EOBs and plan documents in large print, braille, audio or another language at no cost. Call Member services and tell us what works for you.
