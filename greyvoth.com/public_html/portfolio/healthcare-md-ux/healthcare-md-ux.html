HealthcareMD UX Case Study

UX Design  ·  Healthcare Patient Experience

This place was a good practice with a broken website. Patients couldn't schedule online or find the right provider. Further, they coudn't figure out what was covered, so call volumes were high. Staff spent their days answering questions that the site should have handled.

I rebuilt it around what users actually needed with less friction, fewer calls and more new patients!
